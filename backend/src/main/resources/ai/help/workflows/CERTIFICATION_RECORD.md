Submit a certification proof ("{myArea}" > "{certifications}"), e.g. respiratory protection fitness or the recurring driving license check ("{fsk}"). Pick the certification from the catalog, enter the completion date and, depending on the certification, upload a proof document. Only one open request per certification at a time. After approval the proof is on record; validity follows from the certification interval. Certifications granted only through services or operations cannot be submitted by the user.
